## About The Business
Costramp Printing and Branding Solutions is a printing company specializing in custom branding, corporate wear, signage, and promotional materials for small businesses and individuals.

## Website Pages
- index.html - Homepage with hero banner and company overview
- about.html - About Costramp and mission statement
- services.html - List of services (T-shirt printing, Embroidery, Signage, Business Cards, Banners, Mugs, Caps)
- gallery.html - Portfolio of completed work
- contact.html - Contact form and business details
- enquiry.html - Order enquiry / quotation form
