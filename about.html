About Us

Professional Printing and Branding Solutions You Can Trust

Company History

Corestamp is a South African printing and branding company which provides professional printing and branding services for businesses, schools, organisations and individuals.

We specialize in customized products for advertising, marketing, events and corporate branding. The company was founded to help small and medium businesses strengthen their brand through affordable and high-quality printing.

We use modern printing technology to produce high-quality products and are committed to affordable pricing, reliable service and customer satisfaction. We deliver projects on time while maintaining high-quality standards.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

  • To build long term relationships by delivering professional products.
  • To exceed customer expectations through innovation and excellent service.
  • To provide reliable, affordable and high-quality printing and branding solutions.

Our Vision

  • To be recognized for quality, creativity and customer satisfaction.

Commitment to Quality

At Corestamp, we are committed to maintaining the highest quality standards in every project. From t-shirt printing to billboards and vehicle branding, we check every product before delivery.

We aim to help businesses strengthen their brand through professional printing solutions that leave a lasting impression.
